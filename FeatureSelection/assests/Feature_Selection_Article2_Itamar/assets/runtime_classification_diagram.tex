\caption{Feature-selection evaluation pipeline.
  The 102 real-world datasets are first partitioned by the hardness taxonomy of
  \citet{elmakias2025choosing} into \textbf{Easy} ($n=88$) and
  \textbf{Medium} ($n=14$) subsets.
  Within a stratified 5-fold cross-validation framework, feature
  selection is applied to training folds only; the number of selected
  features $K$ and any algorithm hyperparameters are also chosen on
  the training folds.
  Five classifiers (LR, SVM, RF, ET, XGBoost) are trained and evaluated
  on held-out test folds, with AUC and 5-fold standard deviation recorded.
  Results are aggregated under two settings: \emph{All} (all configurations
  pooled) and \emph{Best} (optimal configuration per dataset), then analyzed
  across five resource dimensions using paired Wilcoxon signed-rank tests,
  win-rates, and Cliff's~$\delta$.}
