    \caption{Schematic overview of the Green AutoML Gatekeeper pipeline. The dataset corpus of 102 tabular datasets is processed through two coordinated tracks: meta-feature extraction, yielding intrinsic Problexity descriptors, metadata descriptors, and their joint representation; and FS evaluation, yielding threshold-dependent No-FS and Needs-FS labels based on $\Delta_{\mathrm{AUC}}$. The one-class gatekeeper is trained only on No-FS normal datasets, while Needs-FS datasets are held out for evaluation. Five anomaly detectors are evaluated under a repeated-subsampling protocol, followed by a threshold sweep and a representational ablation study. The dashed gray node (Stage~2: Downstream Selector) is not implemented in this paper; it represents the subject of subsequent research.}
